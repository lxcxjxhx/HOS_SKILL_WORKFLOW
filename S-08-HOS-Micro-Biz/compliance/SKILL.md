# Risk Control & Compliance

## Red Lines (MUST REFUSE)

- Exam cheating (including but not limited to: online exams, final exams, certification exams)
- Paper ghostwriting (can coach and edit, but cannot ghostwrite)
- Malicious attacks (penetration testing only within authorized scope)
- Cheating tools (auto-grabbing scripts, exam automation that affects fairness)
- Illegal projects (gambling, pornography, fraud, etc.)

## Risk Prevention Checklist

- [ ] Confirm project compliance before accepting
- [ ] Retain chat records for dispute resolution
- [ ] Deposit system to avoid unpaid work
- [ ] Written confirmation for requirement changes + re-quote
- [ ] Define after-sales scope and duration (default: 7-day free modifications)
- [ ] Phased delivery for large orders (> 2000 CNY)

## Compliance Decision Flow

```
Client Request
    |
    v
Is it on the Red Line list?
    |--- YES --> Refuse politely, explain reason
    |--- NO --> Continue
    v
Is the scope clear?
    |--- NO --> Ask for clarification before quoting
    |--- YES --> Continue
    v
Is it within your expertise?
    |--- NO --> Decline or refer to trusted contact
    |--- YES --> Proceed with standard workflow
```

## After-Sales Policy

- Default: 7 days free modification after delivery
- Scope: bug fixes and minor adjustments within original requirements
- Out of scope: new features or significant changes (quote separately)
- Communication: always confirm in writing (chat messages count)
